# Code of Conduct

Continentix spaces — repositories, issues, discussions, and security reports — are professional engineering spaces.

## Expected

- Be precise and honest. Say what you verified, not what you assume.
- Critique decisions and code, never people.
- Respect the maintainer's time: search before asking, reproduce before reporting, read the standard before contributing.
- Keep security reports private until a fix is released.

## Not tolerated

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks in any form.
- Publishing others' private information.
- Disclosing unpatched vulnerabilities publicly.
- Spam, off-topic promotion, or bad-faith participation.

## Enforcement

Violations are handled by the maintainer. Outcomes range from a warning to permanent removal from Continentix spaces, at the maintainer's discretion and without appeal for security-disclosure breaches. Report concerns through a private GitHub Security Advisory on `Continentix/.github` marked "conduct".
